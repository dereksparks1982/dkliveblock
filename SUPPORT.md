# DK Live Block: Bug Reports & Support

**Developer support email:** [elderredsoftworks@proton.me](mailto:elderredsoftworks@proton.me)

Please email Elderred Softworks if DK Live Block misses ads, causes a site to break, fails to stop popups or redirects, or has another bug. Feature requests and suggestions are welcome.

## What to include

- **Subject:** DK Live Block bug, missed ad, broken website, unwanted popup, or feature request.
- **Version:** DK Live Block version (shown in Firefox's extension details).
- **Browser:** Firefox version and operating system, if relevant.
- **Website:** The affected site's hostname (for example, `example.org`). Avoid sharing private URLs, query strings or login tokens.
- **What happened:** What DK Live Block did, what you expected, and whether the problem repeats.
- **How to reproduce it:** A few steps, if known.

**Never send passwords, cookies, API keys, account tokens, personal account information or other secrets.** Screenshots and network logs should be shared only if you deliberately review and redact them first.

## From the Firefox extension

Open **DK Live Block → Report a Problem**, or go to **Settings → Help and bug reports**. The form helps prepare a report, including the installed extension version and optionally a website hostname. You can review or copy the report, or open a draft in your email program. **Nothing is sent automatically.** Your email client may require you to click Send.

## Maintainer and release status

Reports are handled by Elderred Softworks. Sending an email does not guarantee an individual reply or a specific fix time. DK Live Block is **unreleased development source** and has not yet completed full Firefox, YouTube/Disney+ or Adblock Plus parity acceptance testing.

For project status and unfinished tests, see [docs/ROADMAP.md](docs/ROADMAP.md).
