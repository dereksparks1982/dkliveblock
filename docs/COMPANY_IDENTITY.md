# DK LAB: Company Identity and Cultural Meaning

**Owner-approved direction:** November 2, 2026  
**Scope:** DK LAB / Elderred Softworks branding; cultural meaning, not development governance.

## What DK means to us

**DK** remains the established Latin-letter brand. Its intended cultural reference is now the Russian abbreviation **ДК**, short for **Дом культуры** (*Dom kultury*), meaning **House of Culture**.

A **Дом культуры** is traditionally a community venue for culture and creative activities, such as performances, music, art, clubs, and classes. That broader creative meaning suits a studio making games, media tools, software, and other projects.

This is an owner-chosen **branding association**, not a statement that the Latin letters D and K linguistically expand to Russian words. Russian readers commonly recognize **ДК** in Cyrillic, but **DK** in Latin letters alone may not immediately convey the same meaning. When clarity is important for Russian-speaking audiences, show the Russian phrase explicitly:

> **DK LAB · ДК (Дом культуры)**  
> **House of Culture**

## What stays the same

- Do **not** rename **DK LAB**, **DK Live Block**, or **Elderred Softworks**.
- The previously discussed visual association with the Cyrillic letter **Ж** is **no longer the intended meaning of DK**.
- This decision **does not authorize a logo redesign**, geometry changes, new language packs, product rebranding, or unrelated interface changes.
- Preserve established project identities and their legal notices.
- Do not imply DK LAB is an officially designated Russian cultural institution or affiliated with a particular cultural center.

This is the **identity/branding record**, separate from the development-only `COMPANY_BIBLE.md`. Future changes to graphics or site-wide branding require separate owner approval.
