Trusted Pages and advertisers

One Facebook Page name per line. Trusted Pages are protected. Marked advertisers are hidden only when the post also looks promotional. Markers can change both lists.

Unlabeled ad inference is ON by default as in the original blocker and can misclassify ordinary posts. Suggested/unfollowed posts and optional sections are OFF by default.
