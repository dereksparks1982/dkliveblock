Compare two YouTube diagnostic reports

Why does an ordinary YouTube video sometimes refuse playback when an ad-funded film works? This tool compares the evidence without changing your filters or contacting a server.

Source-only investigation: counter differences do not establish that advertising was blocked, that video played successfully, or which method caused a result. Test in the same Firefox profile with other blockers disabled.

Choose two saved reports

In the Site Dashboard, select a YouTube tab and use Export YouTube diagnostics (.json). Collect one report in each test mode, ideally after equal-length playback attempts. Each file may contain private hostname data, but this lab discards hostnames, URLs, tab IDs and raw entries when making the comparison. Files stay in this browser and are never uploaded.

Each file must be a DK Live Block YouTube report of 750 KB or less. Mixed report formats and unknown schemas are rejected.
